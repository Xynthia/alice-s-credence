you are a duck. who lost everything from your home and your relationship to your addicition of bread! while waling about your suffering, you get a call for a job. someone lunch has been stole and you have to figure out who it is. while it isn't the most exciting job it does pay the bills, and once you start the job you notice an underlying case of the stolen salami's!

**player role**
the player is here to solve the case of the stolen salami's! talk to character find information and solve the case.

**player goals**
THe player is rtying to find out who stole the salami's.

**player conflict**
the charachters you are talking to, are hiding the information about this case. find other information to get more information from the charachters or find evidence that doesnt seems to match to other people stories.

player choices
the player can choose who gets arrested at the end of the game

player actions
the player can get words of information from the enviorement and charachters. match these together in sentences to solve the underlying question to solve the main case,

resources
words, information, charachters

game events
everytime you solve a sentence with the correct words, the word will change so you get a sterp closer to the final judgement.
